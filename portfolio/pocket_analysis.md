# Candidate interface pockets | Geometry and structural sensitivity

## Scientific question
Which regions of predicted WT and A53T α-synuclein dimer interfaces appear geometrically pocket-like, and how stable are those descriptions across model choices?

## Work performed
Exploratory analysis included interface-contact examination, pocket geometry, ensemble comparisons and P2Rank-related records. These methods can prioritize candidate regions for further investigation.

## Research contribution
The project emphasizes **model selection and geometric uncertainty** when interpreting potential ligand-accessible regions. In a flexible protein, an apparent cavity in a predicted snapshot is not by itself a confirmed cryptic binding pocket.

## Evidence available here
Underlying predicted structures and pocket-derived datasets are retained in the private research archive and are not redistributed in this candidate. This case study describes a method and interpretation, not an independently reproduced pocket discovery.
