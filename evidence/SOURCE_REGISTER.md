## Findings and verification boundaries

The source-record comparison parses each of 12 compound rows and its WT/A53T primary log and pose. First-ranked scores agree for 24/24 records. The comparison additionally checks recorded version, exhaustiveness and grid fields against the retained configuration; actual output is summarized in scientific_source_verification.json. No docking command is run. The extra WT anle138b_out.log is excluded from that primary record set.

A supplied NAC derived table contains five WT and five A53T rows. Its confidence means can be arithmetically recomputed, but the missing original confidence JSONs prevent independent verification of extraction. No structural numerical table is redistributed here.

The supplied corrected ligand SDF matches the digest reported in its historical audit. Geometry validity is not established by that agreement. The historical audit reports atom mapping errors and a receptor-origin comparison; neither reconstruction was independently repeated for this portfolio.

The current source snapshot does not contain MD trajectories or complete simulation/reconstruction scripts. This record does not represent a full audit of every scientific file. The scientific ZIP lacks a root README; no inference about the live repository is made from that absence.
