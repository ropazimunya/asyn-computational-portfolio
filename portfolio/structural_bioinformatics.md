# Structural bioinformatics | WT and A53T α-synuclein

## Scientific question
How do model confidence and interface contact patterns vary among predicted WT and A53T α-synuclein homodimer conformations?

## Work performed
The original research used AlphaFold Server predictions, confidence outputs and structural contact analyses to inspect predicted dimer arrangements. A five-model-per-target ensemble comparison was documented in the private research record. The workflow examined contact-map similarity, interchain regions and sensitivity to individual predicted models.

## Research contribution
The analysis illustrates why inspection of an ensemble is more informative than selecting one predicted structure. Contact patterns and confidence scores provide **model diagnostics**, not evidence of a physiological dimer or mutation-induced binding affinity change.
