# Investigation 001 Docking Methods and Execution Records

**Record type:** retrospective portfolio investigation. **Investigator:** Ropafadzo Zimunya, with AI-assisted record inspection and documentation. **Decision context:** accurate public description of historical work. This is not a regulated deviation or formal CAPA.

## Expected and observed records

The supplied manuscript describes Vina 1.2.5, exhaustiveness 24 and a search center different from the retained configuration. The recovered primary logs report Vina 1.2.7, exhaustiveness 8 and settings agreeing with the retained configuration. The original research records are preserved privately and are identified by archive hash in the source register. No source file is edited as part of this investigation.

The independent source-record comparison checks score agreement for 12 ligands and two target groups. **24/24** primary first-ranked scores agree between execution log, first pose remark and summary. An additional WT anle138b_out.log is excluded by explicit filename rule; no claim is made about its equivalence to the primary record.

## Impact assessment

Matching scores supports traceability among the inspected records. It does not demonstrate that the manuscript's stated settings were used, that a fresh docking run would reproduce the pose, that a ligand binds, or that a predicted structure was used under permissible terms.

The manuscript attributes the receptor to AlphaFold-Multimer/ColabFold; a later historical report attributes it to an AlphaFold Server model through geometric comparison. That conflict is not resolved by a score audit. The public portfolio makes no claim that receptor provenance or historical usage permission has been established.

## Cause and correction

The discrepancy between narrative and records is confirmed. Its underlying cause is **not established**; version drift or transcription are possible explanations, not findings. The portfolio describes the available records rather than repeating the conflicting methods as verified. The manuscript is retained as historical material rather than presented here as a finished scientific article.

## Verification and disposition

The source-record comparison, output hash checks and synthetic regression results provide bounded verification. Software changes have separate requirements and tests. Original MD stability conclusions remain withheld because the supplied historical investigation reports a compromised ligand preparation and the necessary simulation inputs are unavailable for independent reconstruction.

**Disposition:** record consistency finding supported; manuscript methods/provenance conflict unresolved; biological interpretation not established. This investigation is complete as a retrospective evidence assessment and does not close the outstanding scientific issues.
