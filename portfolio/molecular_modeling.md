# Molecular modeling | Docking records and simulation workflow

## Scientific question
How should exploratory ligand docking and short molecular-dynamics workflows be documented and interpreted when studying predicted α-synuclein interfaces?

## Work performed
The research history includes AutoDock Vina scoring/pose records and OpenMM setup and short simulation workflows involving an A53T dimer model and anle138b. Associated activities included preparation, equilibration, log inspection and structural analyses.

## Research contribution
Experience in molecular-modeling pipelines, the distinction between executed parameters and manuscript descriptions, and the need to assess model preparation and simulation sampling before drawing conclusions.

## Evidence available here
This portfolio does **not** include the historical docking inputs/outputs or runnable production MD trajectories. It therefore does not claim to reproduce ligand binding or demonstrate conformational stabilization. The runnable example in this repository is limited to synthetic record validation.
